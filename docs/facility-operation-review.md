# Mapped education and healthcare operation review

Reviewed **10 October 2026**. This supplements the existing healthcare and education location audits; it does not replace their OSM topology/land checks.

All **32 mapped education points** now have an explicit desk-review decision. **17 of the 23 school-tagged points** remain eligible for the default School access goal. Six are excluded because they are closed, serve another purpose, lack primary operating evidence, or have a conflicting current campus location. The nine college points remain tertiary inventory outside the School goal. No institution is called closed merely because it is missing from a directory.

The public overlay is [`data/public/facility-operation-review.json`](../data/public/facility-operation-review.json). It contains every mapped education ID, named evidence sources, retrieval timestamps, archived-file SHA-256 checksums, status qualifications and coordinate qualifications. The 41 source captures used by the overlay are in the ignored `data/raw/facility-operation-review/` archive. Original map coordinates are retained: **no school entrance was independently inspected or surveyed**, and a building centroid is not an entrance.

## Decision rule and practical limits

- A current Ministry school profile, current provider programme/contact page, or a recent event reported directly by a government/university/participating organisation supports a current institution. `open` is the model's desk-reviewed institutional category; it does not certify operation at a particular hour, a vacant place, a particular grade, or an accessible entrance.
- The Ministry directory's published update is 23 May 2025. A fresh retrieval is recorded separately. Directory profiles are useful positive institutional evidence, but contradictory relocation/closure evidence takes precedence. Their undated enrollment counts are not imported as current enrollment or capacity.
- `unverified` means the required evidence for this mapped site was not established. A legitimate institution can have an unverified map point. No replacement point is invented from a building/street name.
- Preschool, primary/secondary and higher-secondary institutions are identified separately in `educationSuitability`. The current School goal estimates access to an eligible institution, not whether its age range/admissions meet every resident's needs. Colleges, therapy clinics and state care are excluded.
- Source-specific exact names, reviewed transliteration aliases, registration IDs and island/campus context are used. Similarly named institutions abroad and vaguely similar names are not merged.

## Reproduce and refresh

```powershell
python scripts/review-facility-operation.py
python scripts/review-facility-operation.py --validate
python scripts/review-facility-operation.py --fetch
```

Preparation validates the archived capture hashes, source URLs, expected primary identities and exact HEMS closed rows before atomically writing the overlay. New/missing mapped education IDs fail preparation rather than inheriting an unaudited eligibility value. Validation checks all 32 records and the 17 eligible school decisions.

Fetching writes timestamped **candidate** captures into the ignored archive and preserves the current reviewed capture manifest/public overlay. Network failure, an HTML challenge, changed addresses or newly changed operating statuses do not silently replace the validated snapshot. A later reviewer must compare changed pages, update the curated decisions/date and adopt the checked capture manifest before preparing a new public version. Fetch time alone is not a fresh operating-status review.

The overlay carries a deterministic content-derived version and per-source checksums. Raw pages are public institutional/government documents whose explicit reuse licence has not been established; the committed assets contain compact review metadata and paraphrased evidence rather than reproducing website content.
