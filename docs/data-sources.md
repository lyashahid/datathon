# Greater Malé public-data baseline

The planning atlas runs from `data/public/planning-baseline.json` without a map token, agency credentials, or a network connection. Its current source snapshot was acquired on **10 October 2026**. Public inputs are cached under `data/raw/` locally; that archive is ignored by Git. The public source manifest records each direct URL, request method/body where relevant, UTC retrieval time, SHA-256, byte size, reference period, access status and reuse qualification. `sourceSnapshotUtc` is the latest successful source retrieval, while the bus API has its own earlier retrieval time.

This is a source-backed planning baseline with explicitly modeled population placement and road paths. It is not an observed travel-demand model or a calibrated prediction of ridership, budgets, safety, or emissions.

## Runtime and source refresh

The checked-in processed baseline is loaded by the Python backend; follow [clone and run](clone-and-run.md) for its Node/uv setup. Original acquisition workbooks, raster and OSM files are not required to run the application. The initial baseline acquisition scripts are no longer part of the maintained repository, so the source table and manifest below are acquisition evidence rather than a current one-command rebuild recipe.

Keep original bytes and their manifest together when reproducing a historical snapshot: a mutable `latest` URL alone cannot recover old source bytes. The baseline version fingerprints source checksums and preparation identity separately from generated timestamps. Missing optional evidence must stay an explicit gap, and a failed refresh must not silently activate unvalidated files.

Maintained expansion preparers and required archive inputs are described in [national data models](national-data-models.md), [official island context](official-island-context.md) and [official sea services](official-sea-services.md). Source replacement and forecast acceptance require the explicit review workflow documented for the relevant dataset.

[Domestic flight connections](domestic-flight-services.md) retain 17 explicit Maldivian connections from the airline's published pair list, joined to mapped IATA airport positions. Destination lists do not establish current departure frequency, a nonstop flight, aircraft assignment or passenger load. No airport flight-board scraper is included.

## What is actually loaded

The original Greater Malé snapshot covers Malé, Vilimalé, Hulhumalé and the Hulhulé airport land mass. Population origins cover the first three. It contains **667 land-clipped population origins, 4,359 road graph nodes, 5,743 edges, 45 mapped institutions, 3,662 building polygons, 99 operator stops, 15 route map layers and 11 timed bus patterns**. Of the institutions, 14 have exact official directory name matches, 29 are OSM mapped, and two restricted/specialist destinations are excluded from general access calculations. The graph has 3,646 retained OSM nodes and 713 explicitly marked interpolation points along existing source segments.

## Preparation rules

**Population:** Malé's four ward resident populations are combined into its physical island total; Hulhumalé phases are combined, while harbours are excluded. Observed 2022 resident counts are Malé 137,238; Vilimalé 6,755; Hulhumalé 65,714; Hulhulé 802. Projected 2026 totals used for origins are Malé 143,553.81, Vilimalé 7,249.96 and Hulhumalé 95,473.97. MBS groups Hulhulé with Thilafushi/Gulhifalhu in 2026/2030 projections, so independent projected airport residents are unavailable, not zero.

**Demographics:** The top-level `demographics` object and each island's `demographics` expose source-backed sex and nationality groups at the physical-island aggregate: observed Census 2022 for all four islands; official 2026/2030 projections for the first three. Each group's values sum to its resident denominator. Age, disability and employment have explicit unavailable records with reasons and source IDs for each island; the archived administrative-island indicator sheets exclude the study area. Hulhulé's projected sex/nationality groups are unavailable because the official total combines industrial islands. All group records have `canAllocateToOrigins:false`: applying uniform island sex/nationality ratios to every grid cell would invent local cohort geography and cannot substantiate an equity-access metric.

Every positive WorldPop pixel is intersected with OSM land, weighted by the surviving land fraction, and assigned a representative point inside that intersection. The retained cell footprints/bounds support the map. The weights are normalized independently to each official island projection. The resulting 2026 scale factors are 1.0068 for Malé, 1.0474 for Vilimalé and 1.3846 for Hulhumalé. The substantial Hulhumalé adjustment is visible in validation metadata. 2030 reuses the 2026 spatial distribution with 2030 totals; it does not predict locations of new developments. The connected Hulhumalé–airport coastline is split across the narrow causeway at latitude 4.203 for allocation; this derived partition is not an official administrative boundary.

**Roads:** Existing OSM node identity defines connections. Geometry nodes are contracted only when topology is unchanged, with a maximum 70 m spacing along source lines to support a 100 m attachment threshold. Long source segments gain explicitly marked interpolation points on those same segments. Walking and driving permissions follow OSM access tags; motor one-way direction is retained for driving. Motor one-way restrictions do not prohibit walking in the reverse direction. No ocean proximity connector, ferry crossing or missing road is invented. Turn restrictions, sidewalks, signals, road condition and actual bus driver routing are not included. Road directions through bus stops are modeled shortest directed paths, distinct from published timings and operator map shapes.

**Institutions:** Join exact normalized names to individual government directory records. Do not match a name such as “Medica” merely because “Medical” occurs in a page. Keep each match's source and official institution class. Senahiya Military Hospital and the specialist drug-agency facility are excluded from general-public destinations. **Vilimalé Hospital is officially listed but has no matched OSM coordinate in this extract**, so Vilimalé hospital access is incomplete; no island-centroid substitute is added. Mapped clinics and schools do not imply audited capacity, opening hours, clinical services or public admission eligibility.

**Timetables:** Join a timing order only when present at every stop in the operator's explicitly published direction. Reject non-monotonic or implausibly long segment joins. Pin the calendar to the retrieval date in Maldives time, rather than treating a remaining-departure API as a recurring feed. The captured response has **287 complete trips across 11 patterns, with recorded stop times from 15:20 to 23:59 on 10 October 2026**. R10, R11, R12 and R15 have map/stops but no complete timings. At the accepted 08:00 default, baseline transit comparison is incomplete; the loaded later window is available as a separate inspection choice. An absent departure is unavailable evidence, not proof that the route operates no service. No reverse direction, ferry runtime, dwell measurement or annual service calendar is assumed silently.

**Costs/carbon:** The baseline supplies raw price anchors only. Bus/stop capital prices, annual operating days, layover, per-kilometre total operating cost, bus energy intensity, grid emissions, displaced mode and ridership response require explicitly labeled assumptions or additional source calibration. Retail prices are not a full cost model. Access improvements may be reported from the supplied network and service evidence; passenger trips, avoided emissions and budget savings cannot be claimed as observed outcomes.

## Verification and remaining acquisition work

The original preparation checked source joins, unique road IDs/endpoints, bounded edge lengths, land-origin placement, population conservation, complete trip/stop alignment, monotonic timings, snapshot-only calendars and SHA/date metadata. The maintained backend tests independently check routing, population allocation and source interpretation. A passing geometry or conservation test does not verify physical operating conditions.

The most useful next inputs are a complete dated RTL calendar with both explicit directions and ferry runtimes; audited entrances and public suitability for hospitals/schools (especially Vilimalé); resident demographics at a valid local grain; aggregate boarding/load counts and travel-demand calibration; operator marginal cost/fleet/energy records; and a verified current charging-grid factor. FollowMe/Avas device GPS, police cameras, MTCC passenger transactions and MACL/MPL detailed agency operations are not supplied or authenticated public downloads in this work. They remain permission-required future inputs, not emulated measured data.
