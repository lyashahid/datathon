# Policy investment release

This release implements the attached policymaker review as a source-aware investment workflow for Greater Malé. It keeps the existing map editor, scenario history and population model, and adds coverage-aware results, affordable investment search, destination requirements, distribution scenarios and supporting public evidence. Reported benefits describe modeled residents and journeys on the loaded inputs; they are not passenger forecasts or a spending authorization.

No booking action, private API key or authenticated access was used for these policy evidence downloads.

`/api/policy-evidence?view=summary` serves metadata/counts with empty FeatureCollections and `featuresLoaded: false`. `?layer=<validated-id>` returns one full layer; unknown IDs return 404. Full, summary and layer responses have different ETags. The local snapshot is independent of upstream portals during planning. Invalid local replacements retain the last validated server snapshot; unavailable evidence is not replaced with fabricated records.

## Inputs still requiring agencies

- **MTCC/RTL:** full direction-specific bus calendars, exact stop times, morning/Friday/weekend coverage, dated exceptions, verified ferry arrivals/crossing durations, aggregate boardings/loads and observed arrival-time reliability.
- **HDC/council:** continuous surveyed walking/boarding approaches, facility entrances, crossings/barriers, pavement conditions, completed/occupied housing units, dates and land/site permissions.
- **Health/Education:** suitable services/grades, public/private admission eligibility, hours, staffing and capacity at matched current facilities; named institution reconciliation.
- **MBS/Elections Commission:** official boundary geometries matching the Census constituency rows and clear subgroup denominators.
- **Operators/STO/STELCO:** verified local route, vehicle, labor, maintenance and energy costs; vehicle efficiency and electricity emission factors.

The public [March 2025 puddling event service](https://services7.arcgis.com/TGjun5cqf5Jwp2Ad/arcgis/rest/services/Event_monitoring_3_March_2025/FeatureServer/0) returned ArcGIS error 499, `Token Required`. No underlying event geometry was fabricated. Historic puddling observations would support explicit disruption scenarios after access and auditing; they do not establish flood probability. The IGMH operator website returned HTTP 403; official utilization data was obtained through MBS instead.

Public readability does not establish an open redistribution license. HDC and official spreadsheets/PDFs have no verified explicit reuse license in this acquisition; preserve attribution and confirm publisher terms before redistribution.

## Verification and human study

The policy-source/API checks passed ten targeted tests covering counts, missingness, cohort reconciliation, rejected coordinates/private fields, retained snapshots, ferry day types, lazy representations and cache variants. Release-wide checks and rendered/browser evidence should be appended by the release reviewer; this document does not certify those outcomes.

The planned first-minute study requires five first-time nontechnical participants who work in, or can realistically represent, policy planning, budgeting or public-service decisions. Use the default English Greater Malé experience at 1366×768, with a fresh local scenario and the same validated data/version. Record role, browser, display size, chosen scope, dataset/model versions and timetable coverage. Give one neutral prompt: “Choose a place needing better access, change a transport service, and explain the benefit, investment, annual cost and carbon tradeoff.” Start timing when the prompt ends. Avoid coaching; record each help request and intervention.

A participant succeeds within 60 seconds only if they identify the selected area's access gap, make a service change, explain additional residents separately from baseline coverage, distinguish one-time capital from annual operations, and explain that emissions can rise or fall under visible displacement/energy assumptions. They must recognize the visible timetable coverage qualification. Record completion time, errors, recoveries and their own wording. The acceptance threshold is at least four of five participants succeeding independently. This study has not been run.

After the timed task, ask participants to undo the change, compare affordable alternatives, identify unknown facility suitability, and find the source/date. Conduct an additional exploratory touch session around 390×844 and 768×1024 to assess drawers, scrolling, keyboard avoidance, drag cancellation, touch targets and accidental edits. Mobile testing is a separate protocol, not a completed validation claim.
