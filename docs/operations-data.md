# Public operations evidence and reviewed notices

The retained public-source bundle adds weather advisories, disruption candidates, dated imagery context and island population evidence. These records do not establish observed congestion, live tracking or an applied road closure. See [national data models](national-data-models.md) for current national coverage.

## Retained source bundle

The processed assets are `data/public/operations/bundle.json` and `source-manifest.json`. Original acquisitions remain in ignored `data/public/archive/operations/`; their former acquisition scripts are not part of the maintained application. The source manifest preserves URLs, publishers, retrieval times, checksums, reference periods, units, coverage and reuse qualifications. Running the app does not require these raw archives.

`GET /api/v1/operations/snapshot` serves the preserved public evidence and current notice-review records. Upstream portals are not dependencies of routine scenario calculation. An empty source response is not evidence of complete coverage or absence of disruption.

The original bundle contains 208 official/local study rows with 119 unambiguous display locations. Unlocated records are retained without being plotted. OSM labels and ferry terminals are approximate display positions, not island boundaries or population centroids. Repeated island names are not resolved by guessing their atoll. Greater Malé subdivisions must not be added to overlapping national rows as a new population total.

## Weather and imagery interpretation

Captured CAP messages retain issue and validity times, severity, polygons and update/cancellation references. The limited source snapshot is not an exhaustive alert history. Advisory exposure does not establish a flooded road or cancelled ferry; those need independently supported, explicitly applied operations. Live model weather and marine context are described in [live map feeds](live-map-feeds.md).

The acquired EOX catalogue includes 2020 and 2025 Sentinel-2 viewing mosaics. They combine acquisition dates and are color corrected; no growth, congestion, road blockage or flood measurement is derived. Preserve attribution and the catalogue's [noncommercial reuse qualification](https://cloudless.eox.at/pricing) when using those assets.

## Public notice ingestion and review

Current endpoints are:

- `GET /api/v1/operations/notices?status=candidate|approved|dismissed` lists records.
- `POST /api/v1/operations/notices/ingest` accepts `source_url`, optional selected `text`, `title` and timezone-aware `published_at`.
- `POST /api/v1/operations/notices/{notice_id}/review` records an approved or dismissed decision. Approval requires an operation type, an existing target ID and an ordered timezone-aware validity interval.

The backend allowlist and bounded HTTPS fetcher live in `backend/app/operations/service.py`. Redirect destinations are revalidated; response size and elapsed time are limited. Supplied excerpts remain distinguishable from publisher-fetched text. Records preserve the source URL, supporting text, hash, retrieval method and candidate/review status.

Extraction uses deterministic rules. It does not confidently geocode an ambiguous street, infer a closure extent or invent an expiry. An approved target must exist in the loaded baseline as a road, service or facility. Notice review is persisted locally; it is a planner's review record, not agency approval. Ingestion and approval do not automatically change baseline routing. A scenario must explicitly apply the reviewed operation and its validity.

Back up runtime notice records as described in [deployment](deployment.md). Do not turn public context records, device traces or aggregate port/airport counts into neighborhood demand or live vessel positions.
