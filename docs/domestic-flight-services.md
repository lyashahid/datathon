# Domestic flight connections

Reviewed on 20 October 2026 in Maldives time. The manually reviewed register, `data/public/domestic-air-services.json`, contains **33 two-way Maldivian connections across 19 airports: 18 Malé connections and 15 regional pairs**. Both directions require explicit airline evidence; a destination list alone cannot create a route.

[Maldivian's published baggage sectors](https://maldivian.aero/flying-with-us/before-you-fly/baggage-policy.html) establish 17 Malé pairs and 13 regional pairs, including northern connections through Ifuru, Maafaru and Madivaru, and southern links through Gan, Kadhdhoo, Kaadedhdhoo and Kooddoo. The airline's [29 January 2026 southern-connectivity announcement](https://twitter.com/MaldivianAero/status/2016903134355083338) supplies Gan–Fuvahmulah and Fuvahmulah–Kooddoo, plus departure patterns for five southern sectors. Its [13 September announcement](https://x.com/MaldivianAero/status/2099172385840910628) establishes Malé–Kudahuvadhoo from 17 September. The [President's Office](https://presidency.gov.mv/Press/Article/36143?term=0) corroborates the southern network's February launch. These are published connections, not guarantees of nonstop service or operation on a selected date.

The January southern weekday/time patterns, December 2025 Muli announcement, and September Kudahuvadhoo daily-service announcement remain separate `published_schedule` evidence with `current_calendar_verified: false`. No arrival time, flight number, current frequency, guaranteed connection or same-aircraft layover is inferred. The older [Gan–Kooddoo timetable](https://maldivian.aero/assets/files/Press%20Statement%20-%20South%20Connectivity_V1_2%20ammended.pdf) is historical context, not today's calendar. Independently supported regional sectors allow geographic hops without inventing through-flight itineraries. [Villa Air](https://villaair.aero/) lists destinations without sufficient current pair evidence; [Manta Air](https://mantaair.mv/destinations) does not publish a usable current domestic timetable here. Neither carrier's older operations become assumed current routes.

`GET /api/v1/planning/air-network?date=2026-10-20` returns connections, ordered legs, airport records, schematic GeoJSON, sources and coverage; omitted dates use Maldives time. Airports join by explicit IATA to the normalized OSM aerodrome inventory. Missing coordinates leave the affected leg undrawn. Actual published stopovers can use `via_iata`; independently published pairs are not combined into invented nonstop or through-flight services. Paths use gentle display arcs with exact airport endpoints, not actual flight tracks. Regional branches have stronger styling than Malé spokes, airport nodes reflect unique connected neighbours, and hovering an airport highlights its connections. Aircraft follow the same cached curves as the rendered lines. Current departures, capacity, ridership and load factor remain unknown, as does congestion: airport-island population is not passenger demand. [MACL's terms](https://www.macl.aero/terms-of-service) restrict scraping; no copied board or hidden schedule feed is included.

Illustrative route motion uses elapsed real time and geodesic distance: **400 km/h for planes and 20 knots for ferries**, without a short looping duration or distance clamp. These uniform modelling defaults are not measured speeds or live ETAs. The plane default is below the [ATR 42-600's published maximum cruise speed](https://www.atr-aircraft.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Factsheets_-_ATR_42-600.pdf). Verified, date-valid schedules retain timed progress; old announcements cannot drive a claimed current flight. Recent provider observations remain separate; the tiny LIVE tag requires an actual timestamped observation, and no live route assignment is inferred from a schedule.

The bounded memory/disk network cache survives production restarts on the mounted runtime volume. Its key includes the selected date, model/source version, register and airport coordinates; source changes invalidate it. Live observations are not stored in this deterministic cache. Focused checks: `uv run --directory backend pytest tests/test_air_network.py -q`.
