# Game interaction review

Reviewed 10 October 2026. This records interaction references and their mapping to the current app source. Browser checks and model timings are recorded separately in [planning QA](planning-qa.md).

## Official references and implemented mapping

| Interaction          | Documented game feature                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Current app capability                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Contextual selection | Cities: Skylines II opens individual line panels with service controls from its transport overview. [Public & Cargo Transportation](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/cities-skylines-ii/features/public-cargo-transportation)                                                                              | Clicking an area, bus stop or bus route opens the corresponding contextual card. Closing it returns to the map. Facility markers show their source-qualified location information.                                                                 |
| Bottom tool tray     | Cities: Skylines II places its transport overview in the bottom menu. [Public & Cargo Transportation](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/cities-skylines-ii/features/public-cargo-transportation)                                                                                                            | Explore, Build bus, Services and Undo/Redo stay in one bottom tray. Detailed settings and evidence open separately.                                                                                                                                |
| Placement feedback   | Transport Fever 2 previews construction costs, flags conflicts and permits placement when no error is present. [Stations and Depots manual](https://www.wiki.transportfever2.com/doku.php?id=gamemanual:stationsdepots)                                                                                            | Build bus displays a valid/invalid placement ghost, a concise reason and a proposed connection. Clicking commits a valid proposal. Validity concerns the acquired service graph; it does not establish a legally or physically buildable bus stop. |
| Snapping             | Cities: Skylines II supplies road snapping/guides; Transport Fever 2 snaps stations to streets, tracks or coasts. [Road Tools](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/cities-skylines-ii/features/road-tools), [Stations and Depots](https://www.wiki.transportfever2.com/doku.php?id=gamemanual:stationsdepots) | Bus placement snaps to an eligible acquired road node and constructs directed paths. Stop movement rebuilds graph-based geometry. This is not a curb, clearance or demolition model.                                                               |
| Live impact preview  | Cities: Skylines II provides filtered transportation info views and editable line controls. [Public & Cargo Transportation](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/cities-skylines-ii/features/public-cargo-transportation)                                                                                      | Placement, stop movement and frequency gestures preview access, journey, incremental cost and carbon results. Frequency commits on release; fleet changes commit directly. Analysis is asynchronous and displays Updating/Preview status.          |
| Cancel and recovery  | Cities: Skylines II cancels a road-building step with right-click. [Road Tools](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/cities-skylines-ii/features/road-tools)                                                                                                                                                   | Escape/right-click cancels bus placement; Escape cancels an active stop drag. Undo/Redo restores committed proposal edits. These are app history controls, distinct from the game's demolition mechanics.                                          |

Colossal Order also describes optional tutorials tied to opened services and panels. The app provides a dismissible short tour and local instructions; it does not implement the game's advisor or progression system. [Tutorials & Advisor](https://colossalorder.fi/news/behind-the-scenes-6-tutorials-advisor/)

The app is a transport scenario planner. It does not reproduce citizen agents, simulated traffic congestion, terrain construction, depots, zoning or full game economies. Population allocation, incomplete timetable coverage and illustrative financial/carbon inputs retain their qualifications in Evidence. Unknown coverage must remain distinct from confirmed poor access.

## Basemap tradeoffs

| Option       | App behavior and limits                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Atlas        | Default locally prepared, cached OSM geography and acquired policy overlays. It requires no remote tile service once the app/data are loaded. Its dated snapshot does not establish current operating status by itself.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Streets      | Keyless OpenFreeMap Positron supplies roads, buildings and street/place text. Imported POIs, 3D layers and sprite-dependent icons are removed; policy overlays remain. It needs an internet connection and retains OpenMapTiles/OSM attribution. The provider permits commercial use but offers no SLA. [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org/), [integration guide](https://openfreemap.org/quick_start/)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Satellite    | Without a key, the app uses EOX's public 2025 Sentinel-2 mosaic: 10 m native resolution, 256 px Web Mercator tiles through zoom 14, then upscaled at closer views. No cost, key or signup is needed for noncommercial use under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; attribution and the license link remain visible. The year describes a mosaic, not a single acquisition date. The older 2016 mosaic is separately CC BY 4.0 but is not selected. [EOX pricing/license](https://cloudless.eox.at/pricing), [specifications](https://cloudless.eox.at/products/viewing), [public capabilities](https://tiles.maps.eox.at/wmts/1.0.0/WMTSCapabilities.xml). Optional `VITE_MAPTILER_KEY` selects MapTiler `satellite-v4` with its own plan, imagery credits and usage terms. Imagery changes orientation only, not the analysis graph. [MapTiler API](https://docs.maptiler.com/cloud/api/reference/) |
| Policy brief | Brief capture explicitly switches to the local Atlas and a top-down overview for either satellite provider. This is an app policy: EOX permits reuse under its applicable noncommercial license, while MapTiler separately limits prints and export uses. It is not a claim that every satellite export is prohibited. [EOX license](https://cloudless.eox.at/documentation/license), [MapTiler terms](https://www.maptiler.com/terms/cloud/)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

Immediate pointer feedback does not mean immediate completed analysis. Previously measured novel proposals took 557–593 ms; cached repeats took 21–36 ms, excluding browser rendering and worker messaging. Sub-250 ms novel analysis and one-minute policymaker comprehension remain unverified. See [planning QA](planning-qa.md) for scope and remaining acceptance checks.
