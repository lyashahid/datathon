Our Revised Rariff Rates

 

  • Fuel Discount of  MVR 0.12/- per unit will be given to the above mentioned categories from 1st March 2019.
  • Failure to pay within the period specified in the bill will result in a fine of 2%, and for every preceding 30 days, an additional 2% will be charged.
  • Any discrepancy in the bills must be reported within 2 days of receiving the bill, failing which it shall be deemed accurate.

    Save Energy Campaign Registration

    Save Energy Campaign promotion will be held among customers who adopt energy conservative lifestyle and reduce the bill during the months of March, April, and May. Customers can participate in the promotion by registering through STELCO website. The procedure will be initiated by comparing the bill of March with the bill of February and it will continue to the next 2 months as well.

    The promotion will run from March to May 2022.Therefore, the total difference between the February and March bill will be compared and March to April and April to May bill.
