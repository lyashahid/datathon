# Public policy context snapshot

The UI loads `GET /api/policy-evidence?view=summary` from the validated local `bundle.json`, then loads `?layer=hdc-pavements`, `hdc-masterplan`, `hdc-trees` or `hdc-accessibility-audit` as needed. Summary counts retain their real values while geometry is omitted with `featuresLoaded: false`. The default API still returns the complete bundle. Representation-specific ETags prevent a summary from satisfying a full/layer request. No public portal is required at planning time. Original downloads are in ignored `data/public/archive/policy-evidence/`. `sources.json` records source URLs, publisher, units, reference periods, retrieval dates, SHA-256 hashes, coverage, reuse status, and transformation version.

```powershell
node scripts/policy-evidence-run.mjs fetch
node scripts/policy-evidence-run.mjs prepare
node scripts/policy-evidence-run.mjs validate
```

`all` runs those three steps. Python dependencies are shared with `npm run data:setup`. `MOBILITY_PYTHON` can select an installed Python. Preparation validates originals and table reconciliation before atomically replacing the compact bundle. A failed acquisition is recorded and the existing validated bundle remains available. Server validation also retains the last validated snapshot after a corrupt/missing local refresh.

## Usable records and scope

- HDC pavements: 311 original polygons, 310 with usable geometry and one empty polygon, Hulhumalé. Footprints are useful for identifying pedestrian infrastructure to inspect; widths and physical conditions are incompletely recorded. They do not create a wheelchair-certified route.
- HDC masterplan: 3,958 original lots and mapped geometries in the acquired snapshot. The earlier review's 3,963 is not the current live service count: independent count and ID queries both return 3,958. Land-use and phase are planning designations. They do not establish building completion, occupied homes, admission capacity, or legally available sites.
- HDC trees: 3,838 points, with source species/condition/measurements when present. Tree records provide audit leads. No canopy coverage, shade availability, heat reduction, or carbon benefit is inferred from a point.
- HDC accessibility audit: 1,319 original observations, 1,316 with usable geometry. One lacks geometry and two are positioned at (0,0); those three are excluded from precise display. Categorical observed entrances, ramps, crossings and pavement conditions are retained, with survey dates. Surveyor/account/security/free-text fields are deliberately excluded. A point observation does not certify a complete path or a whole facility.
- MOE November 2023: Greater Malé includes Malé, Vilimalé and Hulhumalé Phases 1–2. Page 12 reports 42 schools (19 government, three community, 20 private) and 38,836 enrolled students. The app has 17 eligible mapped sites in its separate 2026 location audit. There is no institution roster in this statistics PDF, so the counts are context, not 25 proven missing campuses. Page 10's key-stage subtotal of 37,707 conflicts with pages 12 and 36–37; the conflict is retained.
- Census 2022: 17 Greater Malé constituency rows. Total, nationality and sex reconcile; age groups apply to resident Maldivians only. Matching constituency boundaries are unavailable, so no origin-level gains or neighborhood demographic proportions are assigned from these aggregates. Education/employment originals are cached for follow-on analysis; their different cohort denominators are not silently joined into resident totals.
- HIES 2019: nominal monthly expenditure benchmarks at Republic/Malé/Atolls grain. Excludes imputed rental and in-kind gifts, and includes expenditure-reporting households. Consumption expenditure is not current household income. Transport is broader than public-transport fares. A fare-to-expenditure scenario needs an explicit user-selected historical benchmark and is not an observed local affordability rate.
- IGMH: MBS Yearbook 2026 Table 6.6 publishes IGMH institutional totals through 2025. 2025 year-end beds = 537, out-patient reported activity = 464,783, in-patient reported activity = 15,509, patient-days = 86,547, mean stay = 5.6 days and occupancy = 45.9%. 2024 occupancy is `na` and stays null. Encounters are not unique residents or appointment slots. IGMH/Dharumavantha campus activity must not be added twice.
- Ferry calendars: `service-calendars.json` normalizes four official MTCC PDFs. Vilimalé is effective 21 March 2026 and distinguishes Sunday–Thursday, Friday (12:00–13:30 prayer break), and Saturday/public holidays (17:20–17:30 unspecified). Thilafushi is effective 24 August 2026 and distinguishes Friday. Gulhifalhu is effective 21 March 2026 and explicitly has no Friday sailings. The Hulhumalé document is historical (filename 16 April 2024), requires current operator confirmation and does not publish exact prayer-break boundaries. Opposite-direction departure columns are never interpreted as arrivals. All crossing durations remain null, and all calendars remain ineligible for routing until crossing times and stop matching are verified.
- Public RTL bus/vessel route-detail GET snapshots are cached as operator evidence. They do not publish a verified complete recurring calendar or date-range query. Vessel timing lists may differ from the dated PDFs. No authenticated/booking action or private API key is used.

## Unavailable inputs

Complete recurring RTL bus calendars, ferry crossing times and dated exceptions, official constituency boundaries, verified occupied housing completion phases, and routable access/boarding audits require further inputs. A direct public request to the March 2025 puddling event FeatureServer returned ArcGIS error 499 `Token Required`; historic puddling does not establish a flood probability. The IGMH operator site returned HTTP 403 during acquisition; its official statistics publication was obtained through the MBS yearbook instead.

Public access does not imply an open redistribution licence. HDC and official XLS/PDF sources do not expose explicit reuse terms; preserve attribution and confirm publisher terms before redistribution.
